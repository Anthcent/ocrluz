# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People who digitize books, notes, and administrative documents from phones or desktop computers. They work through repeated capture, review, organization, search, and reading tasks, often handling many pages in one session.

## Product Purpose

Ocryon turns photographed pages into searchable, organized text. Success means users can capture material quickly, understand processing state immediately, recover pending work safely, and find or read saved information without learning OCR terminology.

## Brand Personality

Calm, approachable, and capable. The product should feel light and friendly without becoming childish, and structured without becoming institutional.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble Duolingo. Reject thick 3D button edges, mascot-led hierarchy, saturated rainbow sections, oversized gamification, all-caps labels everywhere, and playful decoration that competes with documents. Avoid generic enterprise dashboards and dense admin tables when a clearer editorial composition can show the same information.

## Design Principles

- Keep the task obvious: each screen foregrounds the next useful action and current processing state.
- Make information observable: summaries, progress, recent activity, and document context should be visible without opening multiple screens.
- Preserve user confidence: pending scans, local processing, errors, and saved results must remain distinguishable at a glance.
- Use familiarity with restraint: standard controls and navigation stay intuitive while composition and visual identity become distinctive.
- Adapt structurally: mobile uses a floating bottom dock; desktop expands the same vocabulary without stretching phone layouts.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Maintain keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic labels, minimum touch targets, high text contrast, reduced-motion support, and status communication that never depends on color alone.
